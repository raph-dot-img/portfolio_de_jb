Privacy policy

Short version: this website is a simple static page. It does not track you, does not set any cookie and does not collect statistics. The only thing that really leaves your device is what you write yourself in the contact form.

01 While browsing the site

The site is made of HTML, CSS and JavaScript files served as they are. There are no cookies, no trackers, no audience measurement tools and no user accounts.

As with any website, the server hosting these files may keep technical logs containing your IP address, the date of your visit, the requested page and your browser. This is how a web server normally works: these logs are used for troubleshooting and security, and they are neither exploited nor cross-referenced with anything else.

No external content is loaded from another domain: no Google fonts, no CDN, no social network buttons. Nothing therefore tells a third party that you came here.

Where the site is hosted

The site is hosted at my home, in France. Traffic goes through a relay server (VPS) at Kyun.sh, in Romania, which handles TLS termination before forwarding the request to my personal server. Kyun.sh applies a "zero-access" policy to hosted data, but can technically process requests in clear text while relaying them.

Kyun.sh therefore acts as a processor within the meaning of the GDPR, solely for routing the requests. Romania being a member state of the European Union, no data leaves the EU at this step, unlike the contact form described below.

02 If you use the contact form

The form is handled by Formspree, a third-party service that receives the message and forwards it to me by email. I do not have any database on my side.

What is sent

  • The name you enter
  • Your email address, so that I can reply to you
  • The content of your message
  • Technical data added by Formspree, including your IP address, used to filter spam

What I do with it

I only use this information to reply to you. I do not sell it, do not share it with anyone and do not use it to send you anything you have not asked for.

Where it is stored

Messages you send go through Formspree's servers and remain stored in my account there, in addition to arriving in my mailbox. Formspree is an American company and applies its own privacy policy, which you can read on formspree.io. If this is a problem for you, do not use the form.

Tip: only write in this form what is needed to get in touch. There is no need to include sensitive or confidential information.

03 Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete the data concerning you. In practice, this means the messages you sent me. Just ask through the form and I will delete the matching entry on Formspree as well as the related email. If you want to access or delete your connections to the server, send an email to my friend who runs the Kyun server at webmaster@canevet.eu, stating the IP addresses used for the connection and the approximate time of your visit.

Messages received are kept as long as the conversation requires, then deleted.

04 Changes

If the site evolves and the way data is processed changes, this page will be updated.
